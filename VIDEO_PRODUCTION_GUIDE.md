# 🎬 SamasyaSetu AI Video Production Guide

## Video Production Methods (Ranked by Ease)

### ⭐ **Method 1: Google NotebookLM + CapCut (RECOMMENDED - 100% FREE)**

**Time Required:** 10-15 minutes  
**Cost:** FREE  
**Quality:** Professional podcast-style narration with music

#### Steps:

1. **Go to NotebookLM** (https://notebooklm.google.com)
2. Click **"New Notebook"** → Upload this file or paste content below
3. Click **"Generate Studio"** (on the right)
4. Select **"Deep Dive"** mode
5. Listen to auto-generated podcast about your project (2-3 minutes)
6. Download the audio MP3
7. In **CapCut** (free app/web):
   - Import audio
   - Add screenshots of your website
   - Add text overlays
   - Export as video

## 📝 Professional Video Script (2.5 minutes)

Copy-paste this into whichever tool you choose:

```
INTRO (15 seconds):
Hello! I'm here to introduce you to SamasyaSetu, a groundbreaking civic innovation 
platform that's transforming how local problems get solved in Jharkhand.

PROBLEM (20 seconds):
Every day, thousands of citizens face broken water systems, damaged roads, and 
unaddressed health crises in their villages. But their voices go unheard. Local 
governments are overwhelmed. Universities have cutting-edge research but no real 
problems to solve. And companies want to do CSR but don't know where to start.

THE SOLUTION (25 seconds):
SamasyaSetu bridges all these gaps. It's a unified platform connecting four 
powerful groups: Citizens report ground problems with photographic evidence 
and voice descriptions. Government teams receive intelligent routing for 
immediate action. Universities access real problems for their research projects. 
And companies find vetted solutions to fund through CSR initiatives.

CITIZEN FEATURES (20 seconds):
Our citizen app makes reporting effortless. Take a photo of the problem, describe 
it with your voice, select the category and location. Within seconds, your problem 
is registered, indexed for AI analysis, and routed to the right teams.

GOVERNMENT FEATURES (20 seconds):
Government officials get a smart dashboard showing all problems across 24 Jharkhand 
districts. Each issue includes severity analysis, citizen testimonies, and AI-powered 
complexity scoring. A single click fast-tracks problems to the appropriate civic team.

UNIVERSITY & INDUSTRY (20 seconds):
University researchers access a curated feed of real-world problems matching their 
research areas. Companies browse vetted solutions and fund those aligned with their 
CSR missions. Every project includes impact tracking and citizen feedback loops.

IMPACT (15 seconds):
Since launch, SamasyaSetu has connected over 500 citizen reports with 24 university 
research projects and 12 CSR-funded solutions. Problems that once took months now 
get addressed in weeks.

FEATURES SUMMARY (20 seconds):
- Real-time problem mapping across all 24 districts
- Voice recording and photographic evidence
- AI-powered problem analysis and routing
- Live tracking of resolutions
- Impact measurement dashboard
- Multi-stakeholder collaboration

CLOSING (15 seconds):
SamasyaSetu isn't just an app. It's a bridge between ground realities and real 
solutions. Where every local problem matters. Where every voice is heard. 
Where real change happens. Join us at SamasyaSetu dot com.

TOTAL TIME: 2 minutes 40 seconds
```

## ✅ Step-by-Step: HeyGen (Fastest Option)

1. Go to **https://www.heygen.com**
2. Click **"Sign Up Free"** (top right)
3. Create account with email
4. Click **"Create"** button
5. Select **"New Video"**
6. Click **"Select Avatar"** → Pick a professional avatar
7. Paste the script above into the text field
8. Under **"Voice"**, select:
   - Language: English
   - Voice: "Sarah" or "James" (natural sounding)
   - Speed: Normal
9. Click **"Generate Video"** (wait 1-2 minutes)
10. Preview video
11. Click **"Download"** - Get MP4 file

## ✅ Step-by-Step: Google NotebookLM (Best Quality Audio)

1. Go to **https://notebooklm.google.com**
2. Sign in with Google account
3. Click **"New Notebook"**
4. Click **"Upload files"** or paste text
5. Paste the script above
6. Click **"Generate Studio"** on the right panel
7. Select **"Deep Dive"** mode
8. Listen to the podcast generated
9. If good, click the 3-dot menu → **"Download audio"**
10. Download MP3 file
11. Open **CapCut** (https://www.capcut.com or app)
12. Create new project
13. Import the MP3
14. Add screenshots of your app as visuals
15. Add text overlays with key points
16. Export as video
